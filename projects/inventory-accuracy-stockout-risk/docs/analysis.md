# Analysis brief

## Business question
Where should operations teams focus first: unreliable inventory records or
items likely to run out?

## KPI definitions
Inventory accuracy measures absolute physical-versus-system variance. Stockout
risk is a prioritization score, not a probability; investigate high-risk pairs
before changing replenishment settings.

## Recommended actions
1. Expedite or transfer High-risk pairs with low days of cover.
2. Recount low-accuracy pairs before trusting their available balance.
3. Investigate repeated variance by location and receiving process.

## Limitations
Synthetic demand omits promotions, seasonality, returns, open purchase orders,
and supplier on-time performance. Replace proxy assumptions with production
history before operational use.
