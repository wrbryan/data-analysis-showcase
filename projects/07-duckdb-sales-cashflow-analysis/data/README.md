# Synthetic data package

This folder contains fictional business data for the DuckDB sales cashflow analysis project.

## Files

- sales_orders.csv: order-level sales data including region, channel, product, units, pricing, cost, and payment terms.
- cash_receipts.csv: receipts allocated to sales orders with collection dates and amounts.
- operating_expenses.csv: monthly operating expense records by region and expense category.

## Notes

- The data is synthetic and meant for portfolio demonstration.
- It covers multiple years of monthly operating activity and includes open receivables and delayed collections.
- No employer, customer, or production information is included.
